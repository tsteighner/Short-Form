Design Beginnings


Design Fuego

Design Fuego was a great way to learn how to use code effectively. There were many opportunities to create uniquely and learn about each other through our presentations.

Logo A/B/C

The Logo A/B/C project enouraged creativity and self reflection. By creating logos that encapsulated who I am as a person, I was able to better understand my design preferences and style.

UX Testing Logo Project

The UX Testing Logo Project allowed me to get feedback on the designs I had made, and see how others reacted to them. Through this project, I was able to create a design that feels creative and useful for future endeavors

Good Design

The Good Design project inspired me to look at designs from a closer perspective and examine the detail put into the art. Through this project, I was able to learn how effective simple design choices can be in creating pieces for future projects.

Course Goals


1. Consider the role of rhetoric, technology, and ethics in acts of making.

When creating, these projects helped me to understand how rhetoric, technology, and ethics play a role in the design process. The "Good Design" project helped me to use rhetoric and technology by learning how to write more technically. Ethics were a component in all projects because of how each design was made respecfully and understandable to the audience. The logo project is where I was able to apply technology to create my own design. Every project from this section utilized all three acts of making.

2. Leverage Design Principles.

Design principles such as color, repetition, contrast, type, proximity, and alignment are all principles learned in this class. Each principle was applied to these projects to successfully create designs that have a purpose and meet the goals set by the designer. Being aware of these principles lead me to create with more detail and purpose. The "Good Design" project focused on analyzing these principles closely. I applied these skills into that project and throughout the rest of my work.

3. Engage with others in a workshop environment.

Engaging with others in workshop environments is crucial when it comes to designing. By sharing feedback, ideas, and offering room for growth, I was able to transform my work into something impactful. By bringing in projects to workshop, I learned how to refine my work and see what is successful and what isn't. The "Design Fuego" project was a project where I relied a lot on workshop, because I learned about coding and I was not sure how to use it prior.



4. Practice revision within and across media forms.

Learning how to revise work through forms of media is a skill I developed from these projects. By figuring out how to navigate different designing platforms, I was able to improve my skills and learn what the class likes to see best. These skills will be important in future careers, because I will know how to adapt to each platform that is needed to create media. Switching from different platforms like Adobe and Canva for the logo project is one way that I learned how to revise work across media forms.

5. Learn to present your work.

Presenting work is a crucial skill that helped to improve my work. Through presenting, I noticed mistakes that I was then able to revise. Talking about why I created what I made shows that I care about my work and put effort into it. Presenting projects also gave my group a chance to connect and give feedback to each other. Without presenting, there would be minimal room for improvement. I enjoyed looking at people's Design Fuego projects, and I enjoyed showing my classmates my work. Specifically, the Good Design project, because we got to talk in small groups about why we picked the design that we did.

Two-page Spread

This project helped me understand the importance of a good layout and design in journalism. I learned valuable skills in InDesign with layers and how to align elements properly.

Infographic

The infographic assignment was helpful in strengthening my design skills. Infographics taught me how to effectively communicate information through aesthetic visuals.

Advertisement

Creating an advertisement was a useful and fun experience for me. I learned how to use tools in Photoshop and erase the background of an image. Learning how to create flyers and posters will be useful in my future endeavors.

Accessibile Design

Creating an accessible design was difficult but rewarding. It taught me the importance of considering all users when designing digital content. The skills I gained through this project will be carried on in my future work.

Course Goals


1. Consider the role of rhetoric, technology, and ethics in acts of making.

Learning how to integrate rhetoric and ethics into my design process has been beneficial. The accessible design project helped me understand the impact of my work on different audiences and the responsibility that comes with creating digital media. Technology played a key role in all of the projects in the short form section. Using platforms like Photoshop and InDesign has strengthened my creativity and gave me the power to create engaging designs.

2. Leverage Design Principles.

Utilizing design principles has been exciting in my creative process. Through the infographic assignment, I was able to apply these principles to create a visually appealing and informative design. Taking the time to understand and implement more design principles has significantly improved the quality of my work. I have grown as a creater, and learned how to make designs that are both aesthetically pleasing and functional.

3. Engage with others in a workshop environment.

Collaboration has been a key component of my creative experience. Working with peers in our workshop settings has allowed me to see other perspectives and improve my communication skills. Through the two page spread, I was given feedback on how to make a better design. Without the feedback I was given, I would have lacked some skills that I now hold.



4. Practice revision within and across media forms.

Revision has taught me how to constantly reevaltuate and improve my work. The infographic project was particularly helpful in this regard. After seeing how my peers took on the project, I saw room for improvement on mine. Through InDesign, I found different ways to revise my wrok with fonts, art, and color editing.

5. Learn to present your work.

Through the presentation of my work, I have learned how to effectively communicate my ideas and design choices. I enjoyed presenting my take on the two-page spread. After presenting this project, I felt more confident in my design skills and how to support the decisions I made.
